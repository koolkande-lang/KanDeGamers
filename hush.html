What you're doing

You are climbing six floors of a toy factory, and each one is locked. The power, two terminals, a door code and the shutter stand between you and the loading bay.

Read every note — the codes are written down somewhere. Find the Reacher on the stuffing floor; it gets you things behind wire.

WINK is in here with you. It hunts by sound. Walk when it's close, and it will lose you.

The tapes

There are six video tapes, one on every floor. Picking one up only puts it in your bag — to watch it you have to find a television, and there is one on every floor too.

That means standing still in the dark with the sound up. Which is exactly when it finds you.

You can get out without watching any of them — but you won't know what happened here.

HUSHABYE TOY CO.

For forty years this factory made the Hushables — soft friends who sit up all night so that children can sleep.

Their biggest friend was WINK.

On the 3rd of November 2014 every person inside walked out through the loading bay and none of them came back. The lights were left on. The machines were left running.

You got in through a gap in the fence. The gap is gone now.

"WINK NEVER SLEEPS — SO YOU CAN."
